EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This file captures 16 exemplar tweets from Charlie Bilello, SubuTrade, Mark Minervini, and Unusual Whales, plus a limitation note for TheTransports. Across the set, the highest-performing formats are: chart + one-line takeaway, anomaly framing, positioning/flow snapshots, and process lessons with specifics. Charlie is the cleanest macro-chart narrator, Subu is the densest on internals and positioning, Mark is the most mentor-like and psychology driven, and Unusual Whales is the most headline-desk/newswire-like.

ACCOUNT STYLE NOTES

@charliebilello
Voice is clean, evidence-first, and chart-led. Typical post opens with a declarative observation, then compresses multiple data points into one sentence or a few bullet-like clauses. Minimal emojis, minimal hashtags, rare slang, frequent chart references, and occasional ticker tags at the end.

@SubuTrade
Voice is tactical, data-driven, and written for active market participants. Posts are usually short and rely on trader-native concepts such as short interest, breadth, put volume, correlations, and positioning extremes. Very little filler. Often states the setup first, then implies the trade or asks whether this time will be different.

@markminervini
Voice is coaching-heavy and behavior-first. Tweets read like distilled trading lessons: discipline, consistency, humility, skill-building, and self-mastery. Punctuation is straightforward, sentence structure is direct, and the best posts feel like hard rules learned from scars rather than quotes built for aesthetics.

@unusual_whales
Voice is fast, urgent, and newswire-like. Posts often begin with BREAKING or JUST IN, then deliver a fact, source attribution, or stat in one burst. Frequent numerics, “per [source]” sourcing, broad topic range, occasional emoji, and a much higher update cadence than the other accounts.

@TheTransports
Insufficient publicly indexed evidence to build a rigorous exemplar set with exact timestamps, engagement, and original links. Excluded from the combined style model for quality-control reasons.

OVERALL COMBINED VOICE PROFILE

Core tone:
- evidence-forward
- concise
- punchy
- low-friction
- written for fast scanning

Typical winning structures:
1. one-sentence headline + chart
2. extreme statistic + comparison to history
3. positioning snapshot + implied forward setup
4. universal lesson + specific behavioral fix

Length:
- Charlie and Subu: short to medium
- Mark: medium
- Unusual Whales: very short to short

Punctuation:
- Charlie: restrained; commas, colons, occasional ellipsis
- Subu: restrained; more trading shorthand and occasional question
- Mark: direct sentences, occasional rhetorical buildup, emphasis through phrasing
- Unusual Whales: urgency markers, all caps tags like BREAKING or JUST IN

Emojis:
- Charlie: almost none
- Subu: very few
- Mark: rare in sampled posts
- Unusual Whales: occasional, especially urgency or headline cues

Hashtags:
- not central to any of the four sampled voices
- tickers matter much more than hashtags

Links:
- Charlie often references charts/video
- Subu often implies chart context and topic threads
- Mark can go viral with pure text
- Unusual Whales frequently posts short source-attributed blurbs with or without chart images

Chart references:
- Charlie: core to identity
- Subu: common, but more for setup confirmation than storytelling
- Mark: not central in sampled indexed popular posts
- Unusual Whales: optional, often attached to stat/news drops

Data formatting:
- Charlie: compressed chronology, percentages, valuation ladders
- Subu: trader terminology, sector ETFs, positioning extremes, market internals
- Mark: triads, commands, rule statements
- Unusual Whales: percentages, broad macro facts, “per [source]”
